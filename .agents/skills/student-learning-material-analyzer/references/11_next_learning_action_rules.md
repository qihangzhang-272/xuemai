# Next Learning Action Rules

## Action Types
- next_lesson_focus
- targeted_practice
- review_protocol
- parent_follow_up
- teacher_observation_task
- student_self_check_step

## Quality Rules
- Actions must be short, concrete, teachable, and linked to evidenceRefs.
- Prioritize one or two high-impact actions over long generic lists.
- Distinguish immediate next lesson from 3-day, 7-day, or monthly follow-up.
- Include verification method: what evidence will show improvement.

## Prohibitions
- Do not promise score improvement.
- Do not prescribe heavy workload without evidence of need.
- Do not generate actions for unsupported diagnoses.
